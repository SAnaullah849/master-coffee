Our Story

Master Coffee was born from a desire to disrupt the mediocre coffee culture in the city. We wanted a place where the SCA standards were not just guidelines, but the law. Our roastery is a laboratory of flavor.

"Coffee is the fuel of the creative mind, and at Master Coffee, we refine that fuel to perfection." — Marcus Master, Founder

As the most passionate roasters in Astana, we travel every year to visit our partners in Ethiopia and Colombia. You can jump to our Contact Section to book a tasting session.
